### Is the "policy association ID" in Npcf_AMPolicyControl_Create the same ID with "UE policy association ID" in Npcf_UEPolicyControl_Create? should I keep both association ID the same for the same UE?

No, the policy association ID from the Npcf_AMPolicyControl_Create service is not the same as the UE policy association ID from the Npcf_UEPolicyControl_Create service. You should not treat them as the same ID.

Distinct Associations for Different Purposes
For a single UE, the PCF maintains two separate types of policy associations, each with its own unique identifier:

AM Policy Association: Created via Npcf_AMPolicyControl_Create, this association deals specifically with policies related to access and mobility. This includes things like Service Area Restrictions and UE-AMBR (UE Aggregate Maximum Bit Rate). The ID for this is the "policy association ID".

UE Policy Association: Created via Npcf_UEPolicyControl_Create, this association handles policies that are sent to the UE itself. The primary example of this is the UE Route Selection Policy (URSP). The ID for this is the "UE policy association ID".

Think of it as two different control channels for the same user. One channel manages how the UE connects to and moves within the network, while the other channel sends specific rules directly to the UE's operating system. Because they are separate associations managing different aspects of policy control, they have their own distinct IDs.
